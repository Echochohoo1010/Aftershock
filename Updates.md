# 🌿 Future Historian - Interactive Policy Storytelling

## What it does:
An interactive storytelling platform that lets you explore "what if" scenarios about AI, technology, and policy decisions through branching narratives.

## Key Features:

### 🤖 AI Story Generator
- Type any scenario (e.g., "What if Australia developed quantum chips?")
- AI creates custom branching stories with meaningful choices

### 📖 Interactive Narratives 
- Choose-your-own-adventure style storytelling
- Every decision impacts the world state (compute power, unemployment, geopolitics)
- Multiple endings based on your choices

### 📊 Real Data Integration
- Live AI industry data (500+ supercomputers, 2400+ AI models)
- Visual charts and timeline tracking
- PDF report generation of your story path

### 🎨 Beautiful Design
- Clean "bento box" layout with everything visible
- Green nature-inspired theme
- Inter font for titles, Noticia Text for stories
- Mobile-responsive grid design

### 🗺️ Story Mapping
- Visual map of all possible story branches
- Track your journey through decisions
- See alternative paths you could have taken

## Perfect for:
- Policy researchers exploring scenarios
- Anyone curious about AI/tech futures
- Interactive storytelling enthusiasts
- Educational discussions about technology impact

## The Experience:
Start with a scenario → AI generates a story → Make choices → See consequences → Explore different paths → Generate reports

It's like having a crystal ball for policy decisions, but way more fun and interactive! 🔮✨
